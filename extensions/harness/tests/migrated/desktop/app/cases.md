- `id` is the parent row's id with a `.cN` suffix, N the case's place in declaration order in the reference file. `source` is the reference path at the pinned tag and that ordinal; the case's own title is not reproduced here. Where a parent row is split (`scope` in the catalogue), its file's cases were shared out among the sibling rows by the words of each row's scope and behaviour; a case may sit under a neighbouring sibling.
